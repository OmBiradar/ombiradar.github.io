OpenSSH is a great tool to connect to remove computers.

The OpenSSH suite supports remote operations with ssh, scp & sftp, key management with ssh-add, ssh-keysign & ssh-keygen, and the server management with sshd, sftp-server and ssh-agent. 

See - [OpenSSH Home](https://www.openssh.org/)
